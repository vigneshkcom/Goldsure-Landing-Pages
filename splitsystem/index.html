What your quote covers

  • The indoor units and system capacity selected for your home
  • One outdoor unit, supply and full installation
  • Licensed electricians and plumbers, no subbies
  • All VEU paperwork lodged for you
  • 5-year ECONOVA warranty

What can affect your quote

  • A double-storey home. Running pipework upstairs takes more time, more materials and safe height access, so we assess and quote for this work
  • The number of indoor units your home needs
  • A switchboard that needs upgrading to take an RCBO
  • Condensation pumps for certain internal-wall layouts
