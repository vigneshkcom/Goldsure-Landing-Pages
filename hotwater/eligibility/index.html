Victorian government rebates

The hot water rebates we apply for on your behalf.

Victoria runs two hot water rebate schemes and they stack. The Victorian Energy Upgrades program (VEU) discounts the upgrade when you replace an old gas or electric hot water system with a heat pump. The Solar Victoria hot water rebate adds a second subsidy for eligible owner-occupiers.

As your accredited installer we lodge the rebate application and the replacement paperwork, so the government subsidy comes straight off your quoted price. Nothing for you to claim, nothing to chase.

Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)
