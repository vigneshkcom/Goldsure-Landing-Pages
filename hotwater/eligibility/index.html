The two rebate schemes, in plain terms

Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)

No income test and no property value test. Applies if your home is 2 years or older and you're replacing an existing gas or electric hot water system. Discount is around $560 to $910 off, depending on the system.

Solar Victoria Hot Water Rebate

For owner-occupiers. Covers 50% of the cost up to $1,000 (up to $1,400 for a locally made unit). Combined household income under $150,000, property under $3 million, and your current system needs to be 3 years or older. One rebate per address, ever.

The two schemes stack on top of each other, plus a federal incentive called STCs (Small-scale Technology Certificates). Every figure here is written as "around" or "up to" because certificate prices move - we confirm your exact number with your quote.

From 1 March 2027, when a gas hot water system fails in an existing Victorian home it must be replaced with an electric one.

Common questions

Can I get both rebates?

Yes, if you qualify for both. VEU and the Solar Victoria rebate stack, plus the federal STC incentive on top.

Do renters qualify?

Renters and landlords can still access the VEU discount. The Solar Victoria rebate is for owner-occupiers only. If you rent, your landlord needs to agree to the install.

Why can't you promise an exact dollar amount up front?

Certificate prices move with the market, so the final figure is confirmed at quote stage, not before. We'll always give you a written number before you commit.

How long does the rebate take?

We lodge the paperwork for you as part of the install. It doesn't add extra steps on your end.

What does the $200 minimum mean?

By law, VEU-assisted installs require the customer to pay at least $200 toward the job. It stops installers claiming a rebate on a job the customer didn't genuinely pay for.
