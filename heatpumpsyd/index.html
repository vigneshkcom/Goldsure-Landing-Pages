The system

The units we install at your home.

290L and 330L to suit your household, in two builds. We recommend the right one for your home layout at the quote.

All-in-one

Tank and heat pump in a single unit. The simplest install and one footprint against the wall.

  • 290L tank
  • Coefficient of performance 5.7 on the base unit, 5.5+ on the booster model
  • R290 natural refrigerant
  • Wi-Fi controls and a variable speed fan
  • Works from minus 7 to plus 43 degrees, with anti-frost operation
  • 5-year warranty on the system, 2 years on labour and ancillary parts

Models ECON-300RVW and ECON-300RVW-2.0E.

Split system

Tank separate from the compressor. More flexibility on placement, and quieter near living areas.

  • 7-year warranty on the tank
  • 5-year warranty on the rest of the system, 2 years on labour and ancillary parts
  • R290 natural refrigerant
  • Built for coastal and harsh outdoor conditions
  • The compressor goes where it suits the house, not where the tank has to sit

Model ECON-300SV-4.2EC.
