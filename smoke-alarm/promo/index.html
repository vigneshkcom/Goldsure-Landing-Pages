Is the price really all-in?
Yes. Our price covers the compliant photoelectric alarms, installation and interconnection by a licensed electrician, correct placement, testing, and your Certificate of Compliance. No good, better or best tiers. You know the price before we book.
What does the 2027 law actually require?
From 1 January 2027, every Queensland home needs photoelectric, interconnected smoke alarms (AS 3786) in every bedroom, in the hallways connecting bedrooms, and on every storey. Most homes need more alarms than the owner expects, often four to six. We assess and confirm exactly how many before we book.
How long does it take, and will you show up?
Most installs take about an hour. We book a specific time, not a four-hour window, and we call before we arrive. A licensed Goldsure electrician does the work, never an unlicensed subcontractor, and you get your certificate before we leave.
